08FAQ

Good to know

Anything else? Write to support@oncourtlb.com.

Is Oncourt free?

Yes. Downloading the app and booking a court are free. You only pay the court price, at the club.

Do I pay in the app?

No. Nothing is charged in the app. You pay the club directly when you arrive, by cash or card.

Can I cancel a booking?

Yes, in the app, within the club's free-cancellation window. You see the window before you confirm.

Which clubs and sports are on Oncourt?

Padel at BlackShot in Byblos for now. More clubs are joining, and the app already supports football, basketball, tennis and volleyball.

Is Oncourt on iPhone?

Yes. Oncourt is on the App Store for iPhone, and on Android through Google Play.

The App Store says Oncourt isn't available. Why?

Oncourt is in the App Store in Lebanon for now. Your Apple Account's country or region needs to be Lebanon (Settings, then your name, then Media & Purchases).

The Google Play page doesn't open for me. Why?

Oncourt is in early access while we test it with a first group of players. Send us the Gmail you use on your Android phone and we'll add you: support@oncourtlb.com.

I run a club. How do I join?

Create a venue account, sign in and send us your club's details from the dashboard. We call you to set up your courts and prices, then your club goes live.

How do I delete my account?

In the app, go to Profile, then Settings, then Delete account. You can also ask us here.
